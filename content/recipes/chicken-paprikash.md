## Ingredients

- 3 Tbsp. olive oil, divided
- 1 1/2 lb. boneless, skinless chicken breasts, cut into large chunks
- Salt and pepper, to taste
- 1 large onion, chopped
- 1 large red pepper, seeded and cut into 1/4-inch pieces
- 4 cloves garlic, finely chopped
- 3 tbsp AP flour
- 1 1/2 tbsp paprika (Hungarian and/or smoked, ideally)
- 1 cup low-sodium chicken broth
- 1 large tomato, roughly chopped
- Hot sauce, to taste
- 1/2 cup plain Greek yogurt
- 1 lb cooked egg noodles, for serving 
- Chopped parsley, for serving

## Instructions

1. Heat 2 tablespoons oil in large skillet on medium-high. Season chicken with salt and pepper and cook until golden brown, 2 to 3 minutes per side; transfer to a plate.
2. Reduce heat to medium, add remaining tablespoon of oil to the skillet along with onion and pepper and saute, stirring occasionally, until soft. Add garlic and saute until fragrant.
3. Sprinkle vegetables with flour and cook, stirring, 2 minutes. Sprinkle with paprika and cook, stirring, 1 minute. Stir in chicken broth, then tomatoes; simmer 5 minutes.
4. Return chicken to skillet along with any juices and hot sauce (if using) and simmer until chicken is cooked through, 5 to 8 minutes.
5. Remove from heat and stir in yogurt. Serve over egg noodles and sprinkle with parsley.

Makes roughly 6 servings.
