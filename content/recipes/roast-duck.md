## Ingredients

### Duck
- 1 whole duck
- 1 head garlic
- 1 lemon
- Herbs

### Glaze
- 1/2 cup balsamic vinegar
- juice of 1 lemon
- 1/4 cup honey

!!INS!!

1. If necessary, thaw the duck in refrigerator for up to 2 days in advance.
2. Preheat the oven to 350, and set up a roasting pan.
3. Remove duck giblets (you may want them for stock!) and pat the duck very dry with paper towels, inside and out. Score the breast skin in a diamond pattern, cutting only the skin and not the meat. Poke the other fatty areas all over with the tip of a knife, especially where the legs meet the body, so the fat can render. Season generously with salt inside and out.
4. Cut the tops off the garlic, and roughly chop the lemon. Stuff them into the cavity with any herbs you want. Fold in ends and tie off legs with twine.
5. Roast breast side up for 1 hour. Flip and roast breast side down for 40 minutes.
6. Pour off the fat and save it.
7. Mix the vinegar and lemon juice in a small bowl. Return the duck to the rack breast side up, brush it all over, and roast for 30 minutes, brushing every 10 minutes.
8. Stir the honey into the leftover balsamic-lemon mixture. Brush it over the breast and roast for 20 minutes more, brushing once halfway. If the skin darkens too quickly, loosely tent the breast with foil.
9. An instant-read thermometer in the thickest part of the thigh should read at least 165°F (74°C). Let the duck rest for 15 minutes, then remove and discard the garlic and lemon. Carve and serve.

Makes roughly 12 servings, depending on the duck.
