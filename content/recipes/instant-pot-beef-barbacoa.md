## Ingredients

### Chipotle Sauce

- 3 chipotle peppers in adobo sauce (individual peppers)
- 2 tsp adobo sauce (from the can)
- 3 tbsp lime juice (fresh is best, or 1/2 a lime, sliced)
- 1 tbsp better than bouillon beef or vegetable base (optional but recommended)
- 2 tbsp cup apple cider vinegar
- 2 cloves garlic, roughly chopped
- 1 tsp cumin
- 1 tsp oregano (mexican oregano is amazing if you have it)
- 1/4 tsp allspice
- salt and pepper, to taste
- 1/4 cup beef broth (reduced sodium is best)

### Barbacoa

- 1 lb. beef chuck roast
- salt and pepper, to taste
- 1 tsp vegetable oil
- 1 shallot, diced
- 1 oz diced green chiles, drained (or any other chopped pepper)
- 1 tbsp tomato paste
- 1/2 cup beef broth (reduced sodium is preferred)
- 1 bay leaf

## Instructions

1. Add all chipotle sauce ingredients (except lime slices, if using) to a blender and blend until smooth.
2. If using a whole beef chuck roast, trim off any substantial fat, then cut into large chunks. Season chunks with salt and pepper.
3. Add vegetable oil to the instant pot and set to Saute. When hot, add beef and cook, stirring occasionally, until browned; about 2-4 minutes.
4. Remove beef and set aside. Add shallot and saute until soft, 1-2 minutes.
5. Add beef broth to pot and scrape to deglaze. Add tomato paste, and green chilis.
6. Return browned beef to the pot, then pour pureed chipotle sauce over the top. Add bay leaf to the top; if using lime slices, add them too.
7. Close lid and seal. Set to **PRESSURE COOK** on High for 45 minutes.
8. When finished, allow pressure to release naturally. When pressure has dropped, open the pot and remove the bay leaves and any lime slices.
9. Remove beef pieces from liquid to a large bowl and shred.
10. Stir together 1 tsp cornstarch with 1 tbsp water until smooth. Add to the sauce in the pot and set to Saute. Cook for several minutes, stirring often, until thickened to desired consistency.
11. (Optionally) crisp shredded beef in the broiler. Move oven rack toward the top. Line a baking sheet with foil and spread out the beef evenly on it. Broil on High for several minutes until charred sufficiently.
12. Toss shredded beef in thickened sauce and serve.

Makes roughly 4 servings; feel free to double or even quadruple for a crowd.
