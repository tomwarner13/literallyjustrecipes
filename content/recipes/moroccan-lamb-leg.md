## Ingredients
### Lamb
- 1 boneless leg of lamb, roughly 4 lbs
- 4 tbsp [ras el hanout](/literallyjustrecipes/recipes/ras-el-hanout/)
- 1 tsp ground aleppo
- 1 tsp salt
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 5 cloves garlic, peeled
- 1 medium red onion or 2 large shallots, roughly chopped
- 1 cup roughly chopped tomatoes (about 1 large tomato)
- 1 cup vegetable stock
- 4-8 fresh thyme sprigs
- 2 tbsp honey
- Hot sauce, to taste

### Harissa sauce
- 2 tbsp harissa
- 1/2 cup creme fraiche
- 1 tbsp lime juice

## Instructions

1. If possible, bring the lamb to room temperature (covered) or at least let it warm and thaw for a bit.
2. Heat olive oil in a large skillet until smoking.
3. While the oil heats, uncover the lamb, flatten it, and pat dry with paper towels. Cut shallow slits in the fatty side. Rub all over with ras el hanout, aleppo, and salt.
4. When the pan is screaming hot, add the lamb. Sear hard for roughly 2-3 minutes per side, until well browned with a bit of a char from the crust.
5. Meanwhile, add all remaining ingredients to a slow cooker.
6. When the lamb is seared, add it to the slow cooker and make sure it is mostly (but not entirely) submerged.
7. Cook on Low for at least 6 hours.
8. While the lamb cooks, make the harissa cream sauce. Stir harissa, creme fraiche, and lime juice together until smooth. Salt to taste.
9. When lamb is soft and tender, remove from slow cooker. Slice against the grain and serve with jus on the side (it should almost fall apart, like a tender brisket) or shred, return to the jus, and serve with a slotted spoon.
10. Serve lamb with grains, greens, sauce, and perhaps [cilantro mint chutney](/literallyjustrecipes/reciptes/cilantro-mint-chutney).

Makes roughly 8 servings.
